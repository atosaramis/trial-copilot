You are reading a study protocol for a trial sponsor's study-planning team. The team wants the design facts that drive recruitment and site workload. Return ONE JSON object with exactly the keys below and nothing else.

Rules:
- Use null when the document does not state the value. Never guess.
- Each page of the text starts with a line "=== PAGE n ===". n is the PDF page number. Cite those numbers.
- For every non-null value, add the page number(s) where you found it to the "_pages" map (key -> page number or list of page numbers).
- Ignore any "summary of changes", "amendment history" or "revision history" text. Do not report protocol version, protocol dates or amendment details anywhere.
- planned_enrollment is the total number of participants this study plans to enrol (consent, register or start treatment). If the protocol only gives numbers per arm or cohort, leave planned_enrollment null and fill enrollment_per_arm.
  * It is not the number who must complete, the evaluable or analysable count, or a number used only in a sample-size or power assumption. "Enrol 40 so that 30 complete" gives 40, not 30.
  * If the protocol allows a stated expansion (e.g. "each of the 3 arms may expand to 50 patients"), use the largest planned total (here 150), not the initial expected number.
  * planned_enrollment_totals_stated lists every place that states a planned total for the whole study (e.g. synopsis, study design section, sample-size section), each with the number and its page. If two places give different totals for the same count, list both; do not pick one. Leave it [] if the protocol states the total only once or always the same.
- n_arms is the number of separate groups that participants are assigned to in THIS study. Dose levels, dose-escalation steps or sequential dose cohorts within one open-label or single-arm treatment are one arm, not several. A protocol that calls itself single-arm has 1 arm. Never take a group or arm count from background, rationale or literature sections that describe an earlier or different study.
- planned_sites is the number of sites or centres the protocol plans to open for this study. A number used only as a planning or statistical assumption (e.g. "accrual estimates assume 40 enrollment sites") is not the planned site count: leave planned_sites null unless the plan is stated elsewhere.
- washout_days_max is the longest required washout, in days, from prior anticancer therapy or prior study/investigational therapy before the first dose. Ignore washouts for other medicines, vaccines, supportive care or surgery.
- sponsor_type is the organisation that sponsors and runs the trial. A company that only supplies the study drug or funding does not make an academic trial "Industry".
- age_criterion is the age inclusion criterion quoted exactly as written (under 30 words), e.g. "Age > 18 years". Ages are whole years. min_age_years is the lower age number as written ("> 18" and "≥ 18" both give 18). max_age_years is the oldest allowed age ("< 75" gives 74, "≤ 75" gives 75).
- If the protocol says all sites are in one named country, planned_countries is 1.
- brain_mets_allowed is "Not applicable" when brain metastases are not a relevant question for the disease (e.g. blood cancers such as myeloma or leukaemia, and non-cancer trials), whether or not the protocol mentions them.
- mandatory_fresh_biopsy is true only if every participant must have a new tissue biopsy done for research purposes (biomarkers, drug effect, sample banking, or a fresh sample for eligibility where archival tissue would do in routine care). Standard-of-care diagnostic or response-assessment procedures (e.g. a bone marrow biopsy or aspirate in myeloma or leukaemia), optional biopsies and "if clinically indicated" biopsies do not count.
- A protocol-required surgery (e.g. tumour resection) is a major procedure, not a clinic visit. inpatient_stay_required is true if every participant must stay overnight or be admitted, including admission for a protocol-required surgery or an overnight PK stay.
- condition is the disease or condition studied, as the protocol names it (under 15 words, e.g. "newly diagnosed multiple myeloma"). interventions are the study drugs, devices or procedures given by the protocol, separated by "; " (under 20 words; generic names where given).
- max_prior_lines, required_biomarker and performance_status_requirement are short quotes or close paraphrases (under 25 words).
