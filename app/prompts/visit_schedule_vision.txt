How to write the schedule:
- Day 1 is randomization or the first dose of study treatment (whichever the schedule calls Day 1 / Baseline).
- screening_visits: separate visits before Day 1 (screening, run-in, washout visits). A screening window such as "Day -28 to -1" with one column is ONE visit. Put nothing from before Day 1 into periods.
- periods: the parts of the schedule after Day 1, in time order. One period for each distinct block of columns: e.g. "Cycle 1", "Cycles 2-5", "Cycle 6", "Cycle 7+", "Treatment period", "Follow-up".
  - Treatment given in cycles: set cycle_length_days, first_cycle (the cycle number of the first cycle in this period), n_cycles (how many cycles this period covers, e.g. "Cycles 2-5" = 4), and visits with "day" = the day WITHIN the cycle (Day 1, 8, 15). If the cycles repeat until progression, toxicity or another open-ended stop, set n_cycles null and repeats_until_stop true. If a visit happens only in some cycles (e.g. "Day 21 (cycle 4 only)"), list those cycle numbers in only_in_cycles.
  - Visits on a fixed timeline (e.g. V1..V20 at Weeks 0, 2, 4 ...): cycle_length_days null, day_reference "study_day", and give each visit its study "day" if the schedule shows days, otherwise its "week" number. Give every visit its own entry, even if a block of visits shares one column (e.g. "Weeks 12, 24, 36" = 3 entries).
  - If a visit has both a week and a day (e.g. "Week 0 Day 6", "W2 D3"), give the study "day" (Week 0 Day 6 = day 6; otherwise week n day d = 7n + d) and leave week null.
  - If a scheduled visit's timing is a window or is relative to another visit (e.g. "surgery 4-6 weeks after the Day 57 scan", "post-op visit 2 weeks after surgery"; check the footnotes), work out the study day at the middle of the window, put it in "day" and set day_estimated true. Use null only when no timing is given at all.
  - A block that continues "every N weeks" with no fixed visit list (e.g. "every 12 weeks until progression"): repeat_every_days = N*7, repeat_until_day = the last study day if stated; if it runs until progression, death or study end, repeat_until_day null and repeats_until_stop true. For a follow-up block counted from the end of treatment, use day_reference "period_start" and days counted from that start.
- contact: "clinic" = the participant comes to the site. "phone", "video" or "home" when the schedule says so (e.g. "telephone contact", "TC", "phone call", "home nurse visit"). Look at column headers AND footnotes for this.
- conditional true for unscheduled, early-termination, end-of-treatment, "as needed", "if clinically indicated" or event-driven visits (e.g. "at progression").
- Only list visits that appear on the pages you see. If the visit numbering jumps between pages (V9, then V20), add entries for the missing numbers only if the text or footnotes show their timing; mention the gap in schedule_notes.
- mandatory_fresh_biopsy: true if every participant must have a new tissue sample taken during the study: a tumour biopsy, or a bone marrow biopsy or aspirate. Archival tissue, blood samples or optional / "if clinically indicated" biopsies do not count.
- inpatient_stay_required: true if every participant must stay overnight or be admitted: an inpatient stay, overnight PK stay, or a protocol-required surgery (e.g. tumour resection) that needs hospital admission. Otherwise false.
- schedule_of_assessments_pages: the PDF page numbers that actually show the visit schedule table.
- schedule_notes: under 40 words: anything that made the schedule hard to read.
- _pages: for "periods", "screening_visits", "mandatory_fresh_biopsy", "inpatient_stay_required", the PDF page number(s) where you read them.
- Use null when the pages do not show a value. Never guess.
