Trial Copilot primary-outcome triage

Did a trial's main question change after it started? Trial Copilot compares each trial's early protocol with how its registered primary outcome changed, reads the amendments and analysis plans, and hands a person a short, dated review queue with page-linked quotes.

A review aid for public trial documents, not medical advice and not a regulatory finding. It uses only public study-level documents from ClinicalTrials.gov: no patient data, and no clinical, diagnostic or treatment advice.
